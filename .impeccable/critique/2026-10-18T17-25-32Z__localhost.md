# Critique: TERRA UTSAV website (home, Find us, team, game, menu, challenge, rules)

**Method:** dual-agent. Assessment A (design review from captures and source) and Assessment B (impeccable detector, CLI plus in-page with a CSP bypass, 20 runs at 1440×900 and 390×844) ran in isolation and were synthesized afterwards. A panel of 10 persona walkthroughs ran alongside. Scored on the 20:49 build of 2026-10-18; a status line after each issue records what was fixed by 23:10 the same day.

## Design specificity verdict

**Authored, not interchangeable.** One calico kitten carries every surface (hero, game sprites, seven team uniforms, results, eggs, 404). Sticker lettering with a chocolate extrude, torn paper notes, a marigold toran with akash kandils, a wet brick with TU in the frog, Marathi greetings marked `lang="mr"`, and the eco rule (stars, no fireworks) kept everywhere. The copy has a voice.

Where it turned generic: the "What's happening" row (stock labels), the invented street map in Find us, a club-directory /team, and above all the products: generated photos, one-line descriptions, no prices. The mascot was the most authored thing on the site; what you can buy was the least.

**Riley (stress tester):** Level 4 blank screen; nav wrap at 1024; detached "My coupons" on /rules; clipped /team chips; egg hint pointing at diyas below the fold; "Supabase" shown to the public; taps within 140 ms silently ignored. *Now: the Level 4 screen, the nav wrap, the detached link and the public wording are fixed, and the egg hint now says the diyas are under the big headline. **Persona panel (10 walkthroughs, same day):** the cheat tester's P0 (one person farming coupons with several accounts) led to confirmed-email-only reward play and one coupon per player. Limiting sign-in to the college domain or adding a CAPTCHA is still the organisers' decision. Priya's P1 (signing in only to be told reward play is closed) led to the game saying "Reward play opens closer to the Mandi" before sign-in. The admin, staff, low-vision, colour-vision, screen-reader, Instagram in-app, parent and mentor walkthroughs led to: IST date pickers and in-browser photo shrinking; a staff console with STOP labels, timeouts and safe retries; a two-tone focus ring; status shown with icons and words; a static accessible tree and a pause control on /team; in-app browser sign-in handling; and the team page kept out of search engines with a takedown line.
